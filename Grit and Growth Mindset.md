# Grit and Growth Mindset

## 1. Grit

### Question 1

Grit means staying committed to a goal even when things become difficult
or take a long time. It is about putting in consistent effort and not
giving up after failures.

## 2. Introduction to Growth Mindset

### Question 2

A growth mindset is believing that our skills and abilities can improve
through practice, effort, and learning. Instead of being afraid of
mistakes, we can use them as opportunities to become better.

## 3. Understanding Internal Locus of Control

### Question 3

**Internal Locus of Control** means believing that my actions, choices,
and effort have an important influence on what happens in my life.

The key point is to focus on the things that are in my control instead
of blaming circumstances or other people. Taking responsibility for my
actions helps me stay motivated and work toward improvement.

## 4. How to Build a Growth Mindset

### Question 4

Key points to build a growth mindset:

-   Believe that abilities can be developed.
-   Accept challenges instead of avoiding them.
-   Learn from mistakes and failures.
-   Take feedback positively.
-   Keep practicing and improving.
-   Focus on progress, not only the final result.
-   Be patient and consistent with learning.
-   Replace "I can't do it" with "I can't do it yet."

## 5. My Ideas to Take Action and Build a Growth Mindset

### Question 5

As a software developer, I want to build a growth mindset by taking
small actions every day. My ideas are:

-   Practice coding regularly instead of only learning theory.
-   Try solving problems myself before looking for the solution.
-   Treat bugs and errors as opportunities to learn something new.
-   Ask for feedback and work on the areas where I need improvement.
-   Learn from other developers and understand different approaches.
-   Keep track of my progress instead of comparing myself with others.
-   Take challenging tasks even if I am not completely confident at
    first.
-   Stay consistent and keep learning new technologies and concepts.
-   When I fail, understand what went wrong and try again with a better
    approach.

I believe becoming a good developer is a continuous process. I may not
know everything today, but with consistent practice, curiosity, and the
willingness to learn from mistakes, I can keep improving.
