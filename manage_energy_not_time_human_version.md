# Manage Energy Not Time – Answers

## Question 1
**What are the activities you do that make you relax – Calm quadrant?**

- Listening to music
- Going for a walk
- Watching movies or shows
- Talking to friends and family
- Playing games
- Playing guitar or doing other hobbies
- Taking some rest or a short nap
- Meditation

## Question 2
**When do you find getting into the Stress quadrant?**

I usually get stressed when I have too much work to finish in less time. I also feel stressed when I am learning something difficult, have deadlines, or worry too much about making mistakes.

## Question 3
**How do you understand if you are in the Excitement quadrant?**

I feel excited when I am interested in what I am doing. I feel energetic, motivated and curious to learn more. I can focus on the task without feeling too much pressure.

## Question 4
**Paraphrase the “Sleep is your Superpower” video – only the points**

- Sleep is very important for our body and brain.
- Good sleep helps us remember and learn things better.
- Lack of sleep affects concentration and decision-making.
- Sleep helps control our mood and emotions.
- Good sleep supports the immune system.
- Sleep gives the brain and body time to recover.
- Not getting enough sleep regularly can affect our health.
- We should treat sleep as an important part of our daily routine.

## Question 5
**What are some ideas that you can implement to sleep better?**

- Sleep and wake up at roughly the same time every day.
- Get around 7–9 hours of sleep.
- Avoid using the phone just before sleeping.
- Avoid caffeine late in the evening.
- Keep the room dark and comfortable.
- Exercise during the day.
- Follow a simple and relaxing bedtime routine.
- Avoid heavy meals just before sleeping.

---

## Question 6
**Paraphrase the “Brain Changing Benefits of Exercise” video – minimum 5 points**

- Exercise can improve our mood.
- It helps reduce stress and anxiety.
- Exercise improves focus and concentration.
- It can improve learning and memory.
- Regular exercise keeps the brain healthy.
- Exercise can help protect the brain as we get older.
- Even a small amount of regular exercise can be beneficial.

## Question 7
**What are some steps you can take to exercise more?**

- Start with a 20–30 minute walk every day.
- Take the stairs instead of the lift when possible.
- Take short walking breaks during work.
- Play a sport that I enjoy.
- Set small exercise goals and slowly increase them.
- Exercise at a fixed time so it becomes a habit.
- Exercise with a friend to stay motivated.
- Focus on being regular instead of doing very hard workouts.

---

## My Takeaway

I think the main idea is to manage our energy, not just our time. Stress can sometimes help us grow, but we also need enough time to relax and recover. Good sleep, exercise, meditation, hobbies and spending time with people we like can help us feel more energetic and balanced.
