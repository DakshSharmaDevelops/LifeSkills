## 2. Tiny Habits by BJ Fogg - Core Message

### Question 2: How can you use B = MAP to make making new habits easier? What are M, A and P.

B = MAP means:

- **M – Motivation:** I need a reason or desire to do the habit.
- **A – Ability:** I should make the habit simple and easy to do.
- **P – Prompt:** I need a clear reminder or trigger to start the habit.

I can make habits easier by keeping the action small and connecting it to something I already do.

### Question 3: Why it is important to "Shine" or Celebrate after each successful completion of habit?

Celebrating makes the habit feel rewarding. It gives my brain a positive feeling and makes me more likely to repeat the habit. The idea I liked most is that what I celebrate, I naturally want to do again.

## 4. Book Summary of Atomic Habits

### Question 5: What is the book's perspective about Identity?

The book says that lasting habits come from changing our identity, not just our goals. Instead of saying, "I want to read more," I should think, "I am a reader."

### Question 6: Write about the book's perspective on how to make a habit easier to do?

The book suggests making good habits easier by:

- Reducing the number of steps needed.
- Preparing everything in advance.
- Keeping the required things visible and accessible.
- Starting with a very small version of the habit.
- Using the "Two-Minute Rule" to make the habit easy to start.

### Question 7: Write about the book's perspective on how to make a habit harder to do?

The book suggests making bad habits harder by:

- Removing the cue that triggers the habit.
- Increasing the number of steps needed.
- Keeping distracting things out of reach.
- Adding friction before doing the habit.
- Using commitment devices to make the bad habit less convenient.

## 5. Reflection

### Question 8: Pick one habit that you would like to do more of?

I would like to **read more**.

Steps I can take:

- Keep a book on my desk so the cue is obvious.
- Start with just 5 pages a day.
- Read at the same time every day.
- Keep my phone away while reading.
- Track each day I complete the habit.
- Feel satisfied by seeing my reading streak grow.

### Question 9: Pick one habit that you would like to eliminate or do less of?

I would like to **reduce unnecessary phone scrolling**.

Steps I can take:

- Keep my phone away while studying or working.
- Turn off unnecessary notifications.
- Remove distracting apps from my home screen.
- Set a daily screen-time limit.
- Make scrolling less convenient by logging out of distracting apps.
- Replace scrolling with a short walk or another useful activity.
